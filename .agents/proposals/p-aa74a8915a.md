A carrier is updated only when a meta-session is opened over it, so its drift is measured as published versions minus spliced versions, never as activity. A repository that adopted the bundle at the then-current version, worked intensively for a few days and went idle falls behind with no obstacle at all: nobody opened a session. The drift of every carrier is measured and reported, and a repository left idle with the bundle half-applied is marked as such rather than forgotten. Learnings an idle carrier lists "for the harvest" wait until a session reads them, so the harvest mark of a carrier is compared with its last work as well as with the bundle's version.

## Evidence

One carrier, one owner. A carrier adopted the bundle at the current version, worked for about three days and stopped; the bundle then advanced by roughly seven versions over roughly seven weeks without anyone splicing them, and no blocker was recorded in its log or roadmap. Its log held a list of learnings marked for the harvest that no lane collected until now, and its harvested mark lagged its last work by about three days. A second repository of the same owner shows the same pattern at a different version. Source: each carrier's session log and roadmap, and the commit history.
