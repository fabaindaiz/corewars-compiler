An agent asked to write a new work from the idea of a published one reproduces, from its training memory, the published work's specific data: its constants, distances and tuning values, even when the code is typed afresh and its comment claims only the idea. Taking only the ideas of a source therefore needs the values chosen afresh, with a reason for each, and a comparison against the published work after writing, not only text written without copying.

## Evidence

One carrier, one occurrence. An agent wrote two instances meant to take only the published ideas, choosing its own constants. A fresh-context review found that the first matched the published work's constants exactly while its comment claimed only the idea, and that the second used a published one's distance. Both were rewritten with constants chosen afresh; the commit holding the copied values was still local and was replaced, so they never reached the history.

What admission still needs: a second occurrence; the literature on memorisation in language models (training-data extraction) checked against its sources. It applies a line of the owner's global instruction file (unlicensed sources are ideas only), so the owner's carriers are one source for the rule; the independent part is the mechanism and its check, the comparison against the source.
