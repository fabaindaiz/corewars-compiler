A claim is measured on the exact artifact the text asserts it of, never on a neighbouring substitute, and a claim about a choice that affects the whole program is measured together with the alternatives rather than reasoned. Measuring the hand-written twins instead of the generated artifacts produced wrong figures and a wrong conclusion that reached a documentation table. A limit called "more than enough" is measured before it is written, and a verifier that samples can pass a very small defect rate for weeks; its sampling rate is stated next to the defect rate it can see.
